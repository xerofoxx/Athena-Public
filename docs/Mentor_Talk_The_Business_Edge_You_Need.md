## 1) What Is Actually Happening

Here is the plain diagnosis.

You are operating as an upstream source.
Others are operating as downstream operators.

Upstream source means you generate the conceptual engine.
Downstream operator means they convert that engine into market-safe language, platform-friendly framing, and audience-facing authority.

You have been assuming this sequence:
1. if I’m foundational, I’ll be included;
2. if I’m generous, I’ll be reciprocated;
3. if the relationship is warm, business alignment exists.

The market runs this sequence:
1. extract value where transfer cost is lowest;
2. simplify language for distribution;
3. centralize authority for conversion;
4. offer minimal ethical acknowledgment sufficient for optics.

That mismatch is where your losses happen.

I’m not saying people are villains.
I’m saying your operating assumptions are mismatched to the game being played around you.

I want you literate, not cynical.

### Authority centralization

### Practicality gatekeeping
Claims like “not polished for camera” can be true and still function as exclusion logic.

### Constraint laundering

### Proposal/delivery split

### Card-holding
Holding back one core mechanism preserves leverage. Common. Morally neutral until intent and effect define it.

Your job is not outrage.
Your job is pattern recognition and boundary enforcement.

## 9) Your Decision Protocol (Use Before Every Significant Share)

Before sharing strategic content, ask:

1. Is this a paid-adjacent context?
2. Is my authorship already publicly anchored?
3. What exactly is being transferred?
4. What usage rights are explicitly granted?
5. What attribution language is required?
6. What commercial terms apply if this is monetized?
7. What happens if they proceed without me?

If any answer is fuzzy, pause transfer.

Pause is not fear.
