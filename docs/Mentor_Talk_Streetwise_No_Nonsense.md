## The four boundary walls

You need all four. Missing one creates leaks.

### Wall 1: Language wall
Define your core terms publicly and repeatedly.
If your language isn’t anchored, other people rename your house and call it theirs.

### Wall 2: Attribution wall
Specify how and where your name must appear.
Not “please credit me.”
Exact language.

### Wall 3: Usage wall
Define what’s free mention versus what requires permission.
Without this, everything becomes “inspired by.”

### Wall 4: Commercial wall
If they monetize your architecture, your terms apply.
No mystery.
No guilt.

These are not aggressive.
They’re adult.

## The one-page pre-share protocol

Before you share anything strategic, run this:

1. Is this paid-adjacent?
2. Is my authorship already publicly anchored?
3. What exactly am I transferring?
4. What exactly are they allowed to do with it?
5. What attribution language is mandatory?
6. What compensation applies if this gets monetized?
7. What happens if they use it without me?

If you can’t answer cleanly, you’re not ready to share.

Pause.

Pause is discipline, not fear.
